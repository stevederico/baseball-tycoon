How to play

  1. Games play themselves. The season runs on its own clock. Pause any time to think.
  2. Read the fan report. After every home game the Park tab shows what fans loved and hated.
  3. Build what they ask for. Pick something in the Build tab, then click a concourse tile.
  4. Set your prices. The Money tab has ticket and menu prices. Cheap seats fill the park; greedy ones empty it.
  5. Build a winner. Training facilities, coaches and free agents turn losses into wins, and winning sells tickets.
  6. Hit all three goals by the last game to win the season.
